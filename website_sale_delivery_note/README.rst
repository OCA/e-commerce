This module lets the customer leave a *Delivery Note* for the carrier
during the e-commerce checkout.

The note is saved on the sales order as soon as it is typed, shown back
to the customer on the address/payment step of the checkout, and it is
carried over to the delivery picking of the order so the warehouse and
the carrier can read it (it is added to the shipping information of the
delivery form).

Usage
=====

To use this module:

1. Enable at least one delivery method in the *Delivery* settings of
   your e-commerce so the checkout shows a shipping step.
2. On the checkout, type any note for the delivery carrier (e.g. a
   preferred delivery time). The note is saved automatically.
3. The note is shown back on the next checkout step and travels with the
   order to the delivery picking, where the warehouse can see it under
   the shipping information of the delivery form.
