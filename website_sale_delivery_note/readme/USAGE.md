To use this module:

1.  Enable at least one delivery method in the *Delivery* settings of your
    e-commerce so the checkout shows a shipping step.
2.  On the checkout, type any note for the delivery carrier (e.g. a preferred
    delivery time). The note is saved automatically.
3.  The note is shown back on the next checkout step and travels with the
    order to the delivery picking, where the warehouse can see it under the
    shipping information of the delivery form.
