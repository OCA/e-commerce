To use this module:

1.  Go to *Website > Configuration > Settings* and enable *Comparison Price*
    under the *eCommerce* section.
2.  Either set a *Compare to Price* on the product form (the field is visible
    when the *Comparison Price* setting is enabled), or create a pricelist
    that lowers the price with a rule that is not shown natively, such as a
    fixed price per product or variant.
3.  Open the product page of the discounted product: the reference price is
    crossed out, followed by the discount badge and the amount saved.

The whole block stays in sync when the shopper selects another variant.
