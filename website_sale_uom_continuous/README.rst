Allows to sell decimal quantities of products in continuous units of
measure (kg, L, m, ...) on the eCommerce.

By default, Odoo casts the quantities added to the cart to integers, so
a customer can't buy 2.5 kg of a product. With this module, quantities
are rounded according to the unit of measure:

- Continuous units of measure keep the decimals allowed by the "Product
  Unit" decimal precision, e.g. 2.5 kg.
- Units of measure based on Units can't be split, and keep integer
  quantities.

This applies to the product page, the product configurator and the cart.

The cart quantity shown in the header counts each line in a continuous
unit of measure as a single item, e.g. 2.5 kg and 3 units count as 4
items.

Use Cases / Context
===================

A greengrocer sells its fruits and vegetables online. Bananas are sold
by kg, at 2.00 per kg, while pineapples are sold by the piece.

A customer wants to buy 1.5 kg of bananas and 2 pineapples. Out of the
box, the eCommerce only accepts integer quantities: the customer can buy
1 or 2 kg of bananas, but not 1.5 kg, although that's how bananas are
sold in the shop and in the backend.

With this module, the customer can set 1.5 kg of bananas on the product
page or in the cart, and the order line holds 1.5 kg, at 3.00.
Pineapples, sold by the piece, still only accept integer quantities:
they can't be split.

Usage
=====

1. Create a product sold in a continuous unit of measure, e.g. kg, and
   publish it on the website.
2. On its product page, set the quantity to 2.5 and add it to the cart.
3. The cart line holds 2.5 kg, and its quantity can be changed to any
   value allowed by the "Product Unit" decimal precision.

Products sold in Units keep integer quantities: setting 2.5 on their
product page, in the product configurator or in the cart turns it into
2.
