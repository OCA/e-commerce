1. Create a product sold in a continuous unit of measure, e.g. kg, and publish
   it on the website.
2. On its product page, set the quantity to 2.5 and add it to the cart.
3. The cart line holds 2.5 kg, and its quantity can be changed to any value
   allowed by the "Product Unit" decimal precision.

Products sold in Units keep integer quantities: setting 2.5 on their product
page, in the product configurator or in the cart turns it into 2.
