A greengrocer sells its fruits and vegetables online. Bananas are sold by kg,
at 2.00 per kg, while pineapples are sold by the piece.

A customer wants to buy 1.5 kg of bananas and 2 pineapples. Out of the box, the
eCommerce only accepts integer quantities: the customer can buy 1 or 2 kg of
bananas, but not 1.5 kg, although that's how bananas are sold in the shop and in
the backend.

With this module, the customer can set 1.5 kg of bananas on the product page or
in the cart, and the order line holds 1.5 kg, at 3.00. Pineapples, sold by the
piece, still only accept integer quantities: they can't be split.
