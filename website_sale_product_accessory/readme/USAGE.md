1. Go to **Website \> eCommerce \> Products** and open a product.
2. On the **Sales** tab, fill in the **Accessory Products** field with the
   products you want to recommend on the product page.
3. Open the product on the website: a **Suggested accessories** block is now
   shown below the product description, listing the configured accessories
   with their price and a link to the accessory's own product page.
