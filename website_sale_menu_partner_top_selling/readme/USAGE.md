1.  Log in to the website and go to */shop*.
2.  Click the *My Regular Products* link (or the configured label) in the categories sidebar or in
    the top categories bar, or open */shop/my_regular_products*
    directly. Anonymous visitors are redirected to the login page.

The page lists the published products ordered by your company (all
contacts of the same commercial partner), sorted by total ordered
quantity in descending order, up to the configured limit. Quantities of
all variants of a product are added together.

Only confirmed sales orders are taken into account. Quotations and
cancelled orders are not counted. If a period in days is configured, only
orders from that period are counted.
