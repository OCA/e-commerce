This module replaces the product category with the eCommerce category in
the side panel of the product catalog, the screen opened with the
*Catalog* button of documents such as sale orders, purchase orders or
invoices.

The panel shows the eCommerce categories as a tree. Selecting one shows
the products of that category and of its subcategories. Only the
categories that have products in the current catalog appear, so a
category without products to show is hidden.

Usage
=====

1. Assign eCommerce categories to the products.
2. Open a sale order and click on *Catalog*.
3. Select a category in the *Website Category* side panel to see its
   products and the products of its subcategories.
