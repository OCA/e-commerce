This module lets you showcase products with video files hosted on your own
server or on a CDN, next to the YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Instagram and
Facebook videos supported by Odoo in the eCommerce media of products.

Such videos are played with the browser's native HTML5 `<video>` player, so
no third-party player or cookies are involved. By default, the image of the
media is shown as a poster with a play button, and nothing is downloaded
until the visitor starts the video.
