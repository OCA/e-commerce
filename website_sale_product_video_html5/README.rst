This module lets you showcase products with video files hosted on your
own server or on a CDN, next to the YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion,
Instagram and Facebook videos supported by Odoo in the eCommerce media
of products.

Such videos are played with the browser's native HTML5 ``<video>``
player, so no third-party player or cookies are involved. By default,
the image of the media is shown as a poster with a play button, and
nothing is downloaded until the visitor starts the video.

Configuration
=============

Video URLs ending with a video file extension (``.mp4``, ``.m4v``,
``.webm``, ``.ogv``, ``.ogg``) are accepted without configuration.

Many CDNs serve videos from URLs without a file extension. To allow
them, go to *Website > Configuration > Settings*, and list the hosts
serving these videos in *Self-hosted product videos*, separated by
commas (e.g. ``res.cloudinary.com``). Subdomains of these hosts are
allowed as well.

Usage
=====

1. Open a product, and add a media in *eCommerce Media* of the *Sales*
   tab.
2. Fill in the *Video URL* with the URL of the video file, e.g.
   ``https://cdn.example.com/videos/product.mp4``.
3. Upload an image: it is used as thumbnail in the product page carousel
   and as poster of the video.

The player can be tuned by adding parameters to the video URL:

- ``autoplay=1``: start playing on page load, muted (browsers block
  autoplay with sound);
- ``loop=1``: play the video in a loop;
- ``controls=0``: hide the player controls.

These parameters are removed from the URL the browser loads, so signed
CDN URLs keep working.

As the computation only depends on the URL, video media can also be
created by imports or through the API.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Generate the thumbnail from the video, as done for the other
  platforms.
- Support HTTP Live Streaming (``.m3u8``) playlists, which need a
  JavaScript player outside of Safari.
